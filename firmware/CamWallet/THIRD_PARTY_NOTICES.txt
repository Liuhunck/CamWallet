1. Waveshare LCD initialization

drivers/lcd.c adapts the initialization sequence from C/01-LCD/lib/LCD/LCD_2in.c.
drivers/board_pins.h uses the pin mapping from C/01-LCD/lib/Config/DEV_Config.h.
drivers/touch.c uses the CST816D register setup from C/01-LCD/lib/Touch/CST816D.c
and CST816D.h. The portrait report decoder and release/debounce review logic are
CamWallet code. LCD and touch share GPIO20; the touch driver does not reset it.
Original author: Waveshare team. Vendor version date: 2025-03-13.

drivers/camera.c adapts the sensor setup and pin mapping from
C/02-CAM/lib/CAM/ov5640.c and cam.h. third_party/waveshare/ov5640_default_regs.h
contains the vendor's default register table, with symbolic register names
resolved to numeric addresses and its license retained. Delay and end markers
are interpreted by the camera driver rather than sent to the sensor.
drivers/camera_capture.pio implements frame-synchronized DVP capture with
32-bit DMA. The main loop rearms capture into a second buffer before displaying
the completed buffer; the vendor's DMA interrupt code is not used.
